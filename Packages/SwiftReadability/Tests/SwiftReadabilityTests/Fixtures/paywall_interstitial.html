Market Rally Extends Into Third Quarter

Stocks extended their gains for a third straight quarter on Friday, as investors weighed easing inflation figures against a mixed batch of corporate earnings reports from the technology and industrial sectors.

Analysts pointed to a steadier bond market and a run of better-than- expected retail data as the main drivers behind the advance, though several cautioned that the rally's breadth remains narrower than headline numbers suggest.
